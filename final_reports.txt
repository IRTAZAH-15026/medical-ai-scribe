**Hello!**  

You’ve been feeling sick for the past three days. You’ve had:

- Throwing up (vomiting)  
- Feeling sick to your stomach (nausea)  
- A fever that’s about **101°F**  
- A headache  

I’m sorry you’re going through this. Here’s what you can do next:

1. **Drink lots of clear fluids** – water, clear broth, or an oral rehydration drink. Take small sips often so you don’t get sick again.  
2. **Rest** – stay in a comfortable, quiet place and try to sleep as much as you can.  
3. **Cool down your fever** – you can use a cool, damp cloth on your forehead or take a lukewarm bath. If you have a fever‑reducing medicine at home (like acetaminophen or ibuprofen) and you’re not allergic, you can take the dose that’s on the label.  
4. **Watch your symptoms** – if the vomiting won’t stop, you can’t keep any fluids down, the fever stays at 101°F or goes higher, or the headache gets much worse, call your doctor right away or go to urgent care.  
5. **Call your doctor** – let them know you’ve had vomiting, nausea, fever, and headache for three days. They can tell you if you need a check‑up or any medicine.

Take care of yourself, and don’t hesitate to reach out for help if anything feels worse. You’re not alone in this. 🌼
